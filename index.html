Dental Assistant: AI Dental Intelligence Platform

My module: CBCT Implant Planning

Before placing a dental implant, a dentist has to know where the jaw bone is thick enough and how far the drill will be from the inferior alveolar nerve. My module takes a 3D CBCT scan and answers that automatically: it segments the anatomy, numbers every tooth, measures each missing-tooth site against published clinical thresholds, and shows the result in an interactive 3D viewer.

0.92 DiceFine-tuned nnU-Net on the ToothFairy3 dataset: jaws, teeth and inferior alveolar nerve
97% accuracyCustom model assigning FDI numbers to each tooth, so missing teeth are located
End to endFastAPI GPU backend serving analysis, 3D meshes and a PDF report

The system measures and reports against published thresholds; the treatment decision stays with the dentist.

Agentic AI · Nov 2026

Khebra: Expert Knowledge Capture Agent

When experienced experts retire, their know-how usually leaves with them. Khebra interviews an expert by voice in polite Egyptian Arabic and turns the interview into a structured SOP. A second bot answers other people's questions from that knowledge.

voice interview → speech-to-text → LLM writes SOP → stored by ID, expert, topic → Q&A bot

The Q&A bot never lists the stored expertise. A user reaches an SOP only by naming the expert and topic, matched with LLM fuzzy matching.
